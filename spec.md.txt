Grovekeeper — spec.md
Overview

Grovekeeper is a mystical garden-building auto-battler where players cultivate enchanted plants inside a sacred grove.
The player manages a large fixed garden grid, placing planters, beds, and ritual containers inside it, then planting magical flora within those containers.

Plants automatically activate during encounters, combining their effects, evolving into spirits, and defending the grove from corruption.

Core fantasy

You are a Grovekeeper, caretaker of a living sacred garden whose plants awaken to fight.

Core Pillars
🌿 Container-based inventory building
🌙 Mystical plant alchemy & evolution
🪴 Nested placement (Garden → Planter → Plant)
⚔️ Automatic combat resolution
🔁 Roguelike runs with discovery
✨ Calm but eerie magical tone
